Important Legal Note:
This calculator is intended as a limitation-assistance tool for proceedings relating to cheque dishonour under Section 138 read with Section 142 of the Negotiable Instruments Act.

For the statutory notice stage, the calculator checks whether the notice was issued within 30 days from the date of receipt of information regarding dishonour.

If the entered Legal Notice Date is beyond the calculated 30-day period, the calculator will stop and will not calculate the subsequent 15-day period, cause of action or complaint limitation.

The actual limitation in a particular case may depend upon the facts and circumstances, presentation or re-presentation of the cheque, service of notice, mode of service, applicable rules, exclusion of time where legally applicable, judicial orders and other relevant circumstances.

This tool should be independently verified before being relied upon for filing or limitation purposes.
