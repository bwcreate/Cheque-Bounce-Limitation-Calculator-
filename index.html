Important Legal Note:
This calculator is an assistance tool for calculating the statutory timeline relating to cheque dishonour proceedings under Section 138 read with Section 142 of the Negotiable Instruments Act.

The actual limitation in a particular matter may depend upon the facts of the case, date of receipt of information regarding dishonour, presentation/re-presentation of the cheque, service of statutory notice, mode of service, applicable rules, exclusion of time where legally applicable, court orders and other relevant circumstances.

The calculation should therefore be independently verified before relying upon it for filing or limitation purposes.
